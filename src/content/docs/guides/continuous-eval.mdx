---
title: "Continuous Evaluation"
description: "Sample live traces and send alerts when checks fail or scores drift."
---

Continuous evaluation runs checks on sampled application traces in the background. Use it to send a webhook or Slack alert after a hard failure or a score drop. This feature is **experimental**, not a substitute for a required CI gate.

The Python and TypeScript `ContinuousEvalRunner` use the same assertion DSL as tests. Give the runner a stable, required `name`: it scopes assertion identities and therefore their history. Sampling controls how many traces are evaluated, not how many application requests are served.

## What alerts mean

- A `hard_fail` alerts immediately, including during cold start.
- A `pass` or `soft_fail` alerts only when drift is detected.
- A `not_evaluated` row is not queried for drift and does not alert. Monitor provider and runner errors separately.

Drift needs at least ten passing or soft-failing history rows; hard failures do not lower the baseline. In 1.1.0 assertion identities changed, so history from older ids starts cold. Judge and embedding checks require their configured provider; their cost depends on that provider.

Use the [SDK continuous-evaluation guide](/sdk-guides/continuous-eval/) for complete Python and TypeScript setup, queue bounds, error handling, alert payloads and shutdown behavior. Python `start()`, `submit()` and `stop()` are awaited. TypeScript `start()` and `submit()` are synchronous, but `stop()` must be awaited to drain work.

See also [drift detection](/guides/drift-detection/) and [configuration](/sdk-guides/configuration/).
